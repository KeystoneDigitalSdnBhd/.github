# Keystone Ecosystem

**Trust is Infrastructure · Attached Yet Detached**

Keystone is a cross-border regulatory technology, sovereign AI, and commercial infrastructure group founded by Owen Lim (Lim Chi Loon), AICB Chartered Banker. It builds autonomous compliance, tax clearance, and civic and financial intelligence systems for businesses operating across Malaysia, ASEAN, North Asia, and the United States.

Clients interact with one AI assistant, カイトー (Kaitō), which connects to 30+ government, banking, civic, and cloud APIs. There is no human helpdesk and no open-ended consulting. Keystone attaches to solve a problem, hands over the keys, and detaches.
